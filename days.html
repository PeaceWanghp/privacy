Privacy Policy

Days (「提醒日」) · Effective date: November 22, 2026

Paid download · No developer account required

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Days (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) handles information when you use the iOS app Days (Chinese name: 提醒日).

Days is distributed on the Apple App Store as a paid download. After purchase, you can use the App’s features without creating an account with us. We do not operate a developer-hosted user database, and we do not sell personal data.

By using Days, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Who We Are & Contact

Developer / data controller contact for privacy questions:

heping.wang@icloud.com

You may also reach us through the developer contact information shown on the App Store product page.

3. Information We Process

Days is designed to keep your content on your device (and, if you choose, in your own iCloud). We do not collect personal profiles, browsing history, advertising identifiers, or analytics events on a developer server.

Information that may exist on your device because you use the App includes:

  • Reminder day content you enter — such as titles, notes, dates, recurrence rules, categories, pins, and related preferences.
  • Media you attach — detail photos or images you take or pick, wallpapers you choose, and demo/export recordings you create.
  • Reminder & alarm preferences — local notification and AlarmKit settings you enable for specific days.
  • App settings — language preference, wallpaper choices, default reminder time, and similar configuration stored locally.
  • Optional iCloud backup copies — if you turn on iCloud Backup in Settings, a copy of eligible day data and related images may be stored in your iCloud container under your Apple ID.

We do not require your name, email, phone number, or payment details inside the App. App Store purchase processing is handled by Apple under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

4. How Information Is Used

Information stays under your control and is used only to provide App features on your device, for example:

  • Showing countdowns / count-ups, lists, widgets, and detail pages.
  • Scheduling local notifications or alarms you configure.
  • Rendering images, wallpapers, and recordings you create or import.
  • Backing up or restoring data when you use the optional iCloud Backup feature.

We do not use your content for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

5. Storage, Widget Sharing & iCloud

  • On-device storage — Day data and settings are stored locally by default.
  • Home Screen widgets — Selected day information may be shared with the Days widget through the system App Group on the same device.
  • Optional iCloud Backup — If enabled, data is written to your personal iCloud storage associated with your Apple ID. Access is governed by Apple’s iCloud service. We do not operate a separate cloud server that receives your backup.

Uninstalling the App removes local App data on that device (subject to iOS behavior). iCloud copies, if any, remain under your Apple ID until you delete them or disable/remove the backup content.

6. Permissions

Days may request system permissions only when needed for features you use. You can deny or revoke them in iOS Settings.

  • Notifications — to deliver local reminders you schedule. Notifications are generated on your device.
  • Alarms (AlarmKit) — to ring an alarm on or before an event day, including when Silent or Focus modes may silence ordinary alerts; you dismiss alarms manually.
  • Photo Library — to let you choose photos for day details or backgrounds.
  • Camera — to let you take photos for day details. Photos stay in the App unless you export or share them yourself.
  • Microphone — only if you use demo/recording features that capture narration; audio is saved in the file you export and is not uploaded to us.

Days does not request contacts, precise location, Health, or tracking permission for cross-app tracking.

7. Analytics, Advertising & Tracking

Days does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The App does not sell, rent, or share personal data for cross-app advertising. We do not use App Tracking Transparency to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

8. Third-Party Services

Using Days involves Apple platforms and services (App Store, iOS permissions frameworks, optional iCloud, widgets). Those services are provided by Apple and governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Apart from Apple’s system services required to run a paid App Store app and optional iCloud Backup, we do not send your day content to other companies’ servers.

9. Data Retention

Because content is stored on your device (and optionally in your iCloud), retention is primarily controlled by you. Delete days or media inside the App, clear wallpapers, turn off backup, delete iCloud copies, or uninstall the App as needed. We do not keep a separate developer-side copy of your reminder content.

10. Children’s Privacy

Days is a general-audience productivity utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children on a developer server. If you believe a child has provided information to us by email, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

11. International Users

Your content processed by the App remains on your device and, if you enable iCloud Backup, in Apple iCloud infrastructure according to your Apple ID and Apple’s policies. We do not operate our own cross-border transfer of your reminder database.

12. Your Choices & Rights

  • Edit or delete day entries and attached media in the App.
  • Enable or disable notifications, alarms, camera, photos, and microphone access in iOS Settings.
  • Enable or disable iCloud Backup in the App’s Settings.
  • Contact us to ask questions about this Policy.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws. Because we do not host your day database on a developer server, many access/deletion requests are fulfilled by using the controls above on your own device and Apple ID.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Days after an update means you accept the revised Policy. Material changes may also be noted on the App Store product page when appropriate.
