隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日 · 适用于 日行（Waynote）

欢迎使用日行。我们重视你的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何收集、使用与存储与你相关的信息。日行以本地记录行程为核心，默认不要求你创建账号。

1. 我们收集的信息

仅在你使用相关功能时，应用可能会在设备本地处理以下信息：

  • 位置信息：用于记录行程轨迹、地图显示、媒体标注位置，以及在你授予「始终」定位权限时的后台持续记录。
  • 相机与影像：用于行程中拍照（含前后摄像头）与录制短视频；照片与视频保存在本机应用沙盒。
  • 麦克风与音频：用于按住说话录音，以及视频拍摄时采集同期声；音频保存在本机。
  • 行程与统计数据：如轨迹点、距离、时长、海拔、活动类型、暂停状态，以及你生成的行程回顾视频等，均保存在本机。
  • 实时活动展示数据：行程进行中，锁屏 / 灵动岛上的实时活动可能显示距离、时长、速度等摘要（由系统在本机呈现，不上传到我们的服务器）。
  • 设置偏好：如地图样式、语言、语音播报、导出清晰度与「地图隐私模糊」等选项，保存在本机。
  • 本地诊断信息：为排查轨迹中断等问题，应用可能在本机写入行程诊断日志，并读取系统 MetricKit 提供的后台退出统计；默认仅保存在本机，不会自动上传。
  • 订阅、终身解锁与试用相关信息：见下方「订阅、终身解锁与付款」。

2. 信息如何使用

  • 提供行程记录、地图展示、语音播报、实时活动与行程回顾；
  • 在你主动操作时拍摄、标注、生成并分享行程媒体（照片、录音、视频）；
  • 在导出视频时，可按你的选择对地图底图做模糊处理，降低地理位置辨识度；
  • 确认免费试用、VIP 订阅或终身解锁状态，以便解锁完整功能；
  • 改进应用稳定性（本地诊断日志；你可在行程详情中主动复制日志以便反馈，复制后是否发送由你决定）。

我们不会将你的行程轨迹、照片、录音或视频用于广告画像。

3. 存储与传输

行程数据、媒体文件与诊断日志默认存储在你的设备本地（应用沙盒）。除非你主动使用系统分享 / 拷贝功能把内容发送给第三方应用或服务，否则日行不会把这些内容上传到我们的服务器。

当前版本不提供云同步账号。若未来增加联网服务，我们会更新本政策并再次告知。

地图底图由 Apple MapKit 提供，可能与 Apple 的地图服务通信；订阅购买、付款与收据由 Apple 通过 App Store / StoreKit 处理。上述与 Apple 相关的通信主要受 Apple 的隐私政策约束。

4. 权限说明

  • 定位：记录轨迹与标注媒体位置；后台 / 锁屏记录需要「始终」权限与精确位置。未授予「始终」时，后台被系统回收后可能无法自动恢复记录。
  • 相机：仅在你拍照或录制行程视频时使用（可切换前后摄像头）。
  • 麦克风：仅在你录音或拍摄带声音的视频时使用。
  • 相册写入：仅在你选择保存 / 分享导出内容时可能请求。
  • 后台音频：用于语音播报及行程中的必要保活，可在设置中关闭语音播报。
  • 实时活动：用于在锁屏 / 灵动岛显示进行中行程摘要。
  • 网络：用于加载 Apple 地图资源，以及向 App Store 查询、购买、恢复与核验订阅 / 终身解锁状态。

你可随时在系统设置中撤回权限；撤回后相关功能将不可用或受限。

5. 数据控制与删除

你可以在应用内查看并删除行程记录及其关联媒体。卸载应用通常会删除应用沙盒内的本地数据（含诊断日志）。通过系统分享出去的文件，由接收方应用按其自身政策处理。

订阅的取消、退款与账单由 Apple 账户管理；卸载应用不会自动取消自动续费订阅。终身解锁权益与 Apple ID 绑定，卸载后可通过「恢复购买」重新获取。

6. 订阅、终身解锁与付款

日行提供 VIP 自动续期包月订阅，以及可选的 一次性终身解锁（非消耗型内购）。新用户可在设备上免费试用 7 天；试用结束后，需订阅或完成终身解锁后方可继续使用完整功能。

  • 付款处理方：购买与扣款由 Apple 完成。我们不会收集或存储你的银行卡号、完整账单地址等支付敏感信息。
  • 权益状态：应用通过 StoreKit 从 Apple 获取当前是否已订阅、到期 / 续费时间，以及是否已购买终身解锁等权限信息，用于在本机解锁功能、展示状态，以及支持「恢复购买」。
  • 免费试用标记：首次启动时间可能保存在本机钥匙串（Keychain），用于计算试用剩余天数；该信息用于试用资格，不用于广告追踪。同一设备重装后可能沿用该标记。
  • Apple ID：恢复购买与跨设备权益同步依赖你登录的 Apple ID，相关账户数据由 Apple 管理。
  • 管理与取消：你可在系统「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」中管理或取消自动续费订阅。终身解锁为一次性购买，不适用自动续费。自动续费规则以 App Store 展示及 Apple 条款为准。

我们不会把订阅或解锁状态出售给第三方广告商。除向 Apple 完成交易所必需的通信外，日行不会将购买信息上传到我们自有服务器（当前版本亦无自有订阅服务器）。

Apple 如何处理支付与账户信息，请参阅 Apple 的隐私政策与 App Store 条款。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不以儿童为主要服务对象。若你是监护人并认为我们误处理了儿童信息，请通过下方方式联系我们。

8. 政策更新

我们可能适时更新本政策。重大变更会通过应用内提示或其他合理方式告知。继续使用即表示你知悉更新后的政策。

9. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有疑问，请通过邮箱联系我们：heping.wang@icloud.com。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · For Waynote (日行)

Welcome to Waynote. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the app collects, uses, and stores information related to you. Waynote is built around on-device trip logging and does not require an account by default.

1. Information We Process

When you use related features, the app may process the following information locally on your device:

  • Location: to record trip tracks, show the map, tag media locations, and continue logging in the background when you grant Always location access.
  • Camera & imagery: to take photos (including front/rear capture) and record short in-ride videos; photos and videos are stored in the app sandbox.
  • Microphone & audio: for press-to-talk recordings and for capturing sound while filming video; audio is stored on device.
  • Trip & stats data: track points, distance, duration, altitude, activity type, pause state, generated highlight videos, and similar data stored on device.
  • Live Activity display data: while a trip is active, Lock Screen / Dynamic Island Live Activities may show distance, duration, speed, and similar summaries (rendered by the system on device; not uploaded to our servers).
  • Preferences: map style, language, voice announcements, export clarity, map privacy blur, and similar options stored on device.
  • On-device diagnostics: to investigate issues such as interrupted tracks, the app may write local ride diagnostic logs and read MetricKit background-exit statistics; these stay on device by default and are not uploaded automatically.
  • Subscription, lifetime unlock & trial information: see “Subscriptions, Lifetime Unlock & Payments” below.

2. How We Use Information

  • to provide trip recording, maps, voice announcements, Live Activities, and trip review;
  • to capture, tag, generate, and share trip media (photos, audio, video) when you choose to;
  • to optionally blur map basemaps in exported videos when you enable map privacy blur;
  • to verify free-trial, VIP subscription, or lifetime-unlock access so full features can be unlocked;
  • to help diagnose stability issues (local diagnostic logs; you may copy a log from trip details to send feedback—whether you send it is up to you).

We do not use your tracks, photos, recordings, or videos for advertising profiles.

3. Storage & Transfer

Trip data, media, and diagnostic logs are stored locally in the app sandbox by default. Waynote does not upload this content to our servers unless you actively share or copy it through the system share sheet / pasteboard to another app or service.

This version does not offer cloud sync accounts. If we add online services later, we will update this policy and notify you.

Map basemaps are provided by Apple MapKit and may communicate with Apple’s map services. Subscription purchases, billing, and receipts are processed by Apple through the App Store / StoreKit. Those Apple-related communications are primarily governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

4. Permissions

  • Location: for track recording and tagging media; Always access and Precise Location help keep logging accurate while locked or backgrounded. Without Always access, tracking may not resume automatically if the system terminates the app in the background.
  • Camera: only when you take photos or record in-ride video (front/rear cameras may be used).
  • Microphone: only when you record audio or capture video with sound.
  • Photo Library (add): only if you choose to save or share exported content.
  • Background audio: for voice announcements and related keep-alive during a trip; you can turn voice announcements off in Settings.
  • Live Activities: to show an in-progress trip summary on the Lock Screen / Dynamic Island.
  • Network: to load Apple Maps resources and to look up, purchase, restore, and verify subscription / lifetime-unlock status with the App Store.

You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings at any time; related features may stop working.

5. Your Controls & Deletion

You can review and delete trips and their associated media in the app. Uninstalling the app typically removes sandbox data (including diagnostic logs). Content you shared elsewhere is governed by the receiving app’s policies.

Canceling, refunds, and billing for subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel an auto-renewing subscription. Lifetime unlock is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored via Restore Purchases after reinstall.

6. Subscriptions, Lifetime Unlock & Payments

Waynote offers a VIP auto-renewable monthly subscription and an optional one-time lifetime unlock (non-consumable). New users may use a 7-day free trial on device; after the trial ends, a subscription or lifetime unlock is required to keep using full features.

  • Payment processor: Purchases and charges are handled by Apple. We do not collect or store your card number or full billing address.
  • Entitlement status: Through StoreKit, the app receives entitlement details from Apple (such as whether VIP is active, renewal/expiration timing, and whether lifetime unlock was purchased) to unlock features on device, show status in Settings, and support Restore Purchases.
  • Free-trial marker: The first-launch timestamp may be stored in the device Keychain to calculate remaining trial days. It is used only for trial eligibility, not for advertising. Reinstalling on the same device may keep using that marker.
  • Apple ID: Restore Purchases and cross-device entitlement sync depend on the Apple ID you are signed in with; that account data is managed by Apple.
  • Manage & cancel: You can manage or cancel auto-renewing subscriptions in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase and does not auto-renew. Auto-renewal terms follow what the App Store shows and Apple’s policies.

We do not sell subscription or unlock status to advertisers. Aside from communications required to complete transactions with Apple, Waynote does not upload purchase information to our own servers (this version has no first-party subscription backend).

For how Apple handles payments and account data, see Apple’s Privacy Policy and App Store terms.

7. Children

Waynote is not directed at children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe information about a child was processed in error, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated in-app or by other reasonable means. Continued use means you acknowledge the updated policy.

9. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at heping.wang@icloud.com.
