隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 12 日 · 适用于 日行（Waynote）

欢迎使用日行。我们重视你的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何收集、使用与存储与你相关的信息。日行以本地记录行程为核心，默认不要求你创建账号。

1. 我们收集的信息

仅在你使用相关功能时，应用可能会在设备本地处理以下信息：

  • 位置信息：用于记录行程轨迹、地图显示与后台持续记录（在你授予「始终」定位权限时）。
  • 相机与照片：用于行程中拍照，照片保存在本机应用沙盒。
  • 麦克风与录音：用于按住说话录音，音频保存在本机。
  • 运动与行程数据：如轨迹点、距离、时长、海拔、导出视频等，均保存在本机。
  • 设置偏好：如地图样式、语言、语音播报开关等，保存在本机。
  • 订阅与试用相关信息：见下方「订阅与付款」。

2. 信息如何使用

  • 提供行程记录、地图展示、语音播报与行程回顾；
  • 在你主动操作时生成并分享行程视频；
  • 确认免费试用与 VIP 订阅权限，以便解锁完整功能；
  • 改进应用稳定性（例如可选的本地诊断日志，仅保存在本机）。

我们不会将你的行程轨迹、照片或录音用于广告画像。

3. 存储与传输

行程数据、媒体文件默认存储在你的设备本地（应用沙盒）。除非你主动使用系统分享功能把内容发送给第三方应用或服务，否则日行不会把这些内容上传到我们的服务器。

当前版本不提供云同步账号。若未来增加联网服务，我们会更新本政策并再次告知。

订阅购买、付款与收据由 Apple 通过 App Store / StoreKit 处理；相关交易信息主要受 Apple 的隐私政策约束。

4. 权限说明

  • 定位：记录轨迹；后台/锁屏记录需要「始终」权限与精确位置。
  • 相机 / 麦克风：仅在你拍照或录音时使用。
  • 相册写入：仅在你选择保存/分享导出内容时可能请求。
  • 后台音频：用于语音播报及行程中的必要保活，可在设置中关闭语音播报。
  • 网络：用于向 App Store 查询、购买、恢复与核验订阅状态。

你可随时在系统设置中撤回权限；撤回后相关功能将不可用或受限。

5. 数据控制与删除

你可以在应用内查看并删除行程记录。卸载应用通常会删除应用沙盒内的本地数据。通过系统分享出去的文件，由接收方应用按其自身政策处理。

订阅的取消、退款与账单由 Apple 账户管理；卸载应用不会自动取消自动续费订阅。

6. 订阅与付款

日行提供 VIP 自动续期包月订阅。新用户可在设备上免费试用一段时间；试用结束后需订阅方可继续使用完整功能。

  • 付款处理方：购买与扣款由 Apple 完成。我们不会收集或存储你的银行卡号、完整账单地址等支付敏感信息。
  • 订阅状态：应用通过 StoreKit 从 Apple 获取当前是否已订阅、到期/续费时间等权限信息，用于在本机解锁功能、展示状态，以及支持「恢复购买」。
  • 免费试用标记：首次启动时间可能保存在本机钥匙串（Keychain），用于计算试用剩余天数；该信息用于试用资格，不用于广告追踪。
  • Apple ID：恢复购买与跨设备权益同步依赖你登录的 Apple ID，相关账户数据由 Apple 管理。
  • 管理与取消：你可在系统「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」中管理或取消订阅。自动续费规则以 App Store 展示及 Apple 条款为准。

我们不会把订阅状态出售给第三方广告商。除向 Apple 完成交易所必需的通信外，日行不会将订阅信息上传到我们自有服务器（当前版本亦无自有订阅服务器）。

Apple 如何处理支付与账户信息，请参阅 Apple 的隐私政策与 App Store 条款。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不以儿童为主要服务对象。若你是监护人并认为我们误处理了儿童信息，请通过下方方式联系我们。

8. 政策更新

我们可能适时更新本政策。重大变更会通过应用内提示或其他合理方式告知。继续使用即表示你知悉更新后的政策。

9. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有疑问，请通过邮箱联系我们：heping.wang@icloud.com。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026 · For Waynote (日行)

Welcome to Waynote. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the app collects, uses, and stores information related to you. Waynote is built around on-device trip logging and does not require an account by default.

1. Information We Process

When you use related features, the app may process the following information locally on your device:

  • Location: to record trip tracks, show the map, and continue logging in the background when you grant Always location access.
  • Camera & photos: to capture photos during a trip; photos are stored in the app sandbox.
  • Microphone & audio: for press-to-talk recordings stored on device.
  • Trip & motion data: track points, distance, duration, altitude, exported videos, and similar data stored on device.
  • Preferences: map style, language, voice announcement settings, and similar options stored on device.
  • Subscription & trial information: see “Subscriptions & Payments” below.

2. How We Use Information

  • to provide trip recording, maps, voice announcements, and trip review;
  • to generate and share trip videos when you choose to;
  • to verify free-trial and VIP subscription access so full features can be unlocked;
  • to help diagnose stability issues (optional local diagnostic logs stay on device).

We do not use your tracks, photos, or recordings for advertising profiles.

3. Storage & Transfer

Trip data and media are stored locally in the app sandbox by default. Waynote does not upload this content to our servers unless you actively share it through the system share sheet to another app or service.

This version does not offer cloud sync accounts. If we add online services later, we will update this policy and notify you.

Subscription purchases, billing, and receipts are processed by Apple through the App Store / StoreKit. Those transaction details are primarily governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

4. Permissions

  • Location: for track recording; Always access and Precise Location help keep logging accurate while locked or backgrounded.
  • Camera / Microphone: only when you take photos or record audio.
  • Photo Library (add): only if you choose to save or share exported content.
  • Background audio: for voice announcements and related keep-alive during a trip; you can turn voice announcements off in Settings.
  • Network: to look up, purchase, restore, and verify subscription status with the App Store.

You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings at any time; related features may stop working.

5. Your Controls & Deletion

You can review and delete trips in the app. Uninstalling the app typically removes sandbox data. Content you shared elsewhere is governed by the receiving app’s policies.

Canceling, refunds, and billing for subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel an auto-renewing subscription.

6. Subscriptions & Payments

Waynote offers a VIP auto-renewable monthly subscription. New users may use a free trial on device; after the trial ends, a subscription is required to keep using full features.

  • Payment processor: Purchases and charges are handled by Apple. We do not collect or store your card number or full billing address.
  • Subscription status: Through StoreKit, the app receives entitlement details from Apple (such as whether VIP is active and renewal/expiration timing) to unlock features on device, show status in Settings, and support Restore Purchases.
  • Free-trial marker: The first-launch timestamp may be stored in the device Keychain to calculate remaining trial days. It is used only for trial eligibility, not for advertising.
  • Apple ID: Restore Purchases and cross-device entitlement sync depend on the Apple ID you are signed in with; that account data is managed by Apple.
  • Manage & cancel: You can manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Auto-renewal terms follow what the App Store shows and Apple’s policies.

We do not sell subscription status to advertisers. Aside from communications required to complete transactions with Apple, Waynote does not upload subscription information to our own servers (this version has no first-party subscription backend).

For how Apple handles payments and account data, see Apple’s Privacy Policy and App Store terms.

7. Children

Waynote is not directed at children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe information about a child was processed in error, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated in-app or by other reasonable means. Continued use means you acknowledge the updated policy.

9. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at heping.wang@icloud.com.
