Second Camera Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

About Second Camera

Second Camera is a separate library on your device. It works alongside the system Camera and Photos apps: you can keep selected photos and videos here without mixing them into your main camera roll.

1. Information We Collect

We store photos and videos you capture or import only on your device. We do not upload your media to our servers.

2. Permissions

  • Camera — capture photos and videos for this separate library.
  • Microphone — record audio while filming video.
  • Photo Library — import from the system library, save back when you choose, and share through the system share sheet.

3. Data Storage

Media is saved in the app’s Documents folder. You can view local storage usage and counts in Settings.

4. Third-Party Sharing

When you share content, it is handled by the iOS share sheet and the app you choose.

5. Contact

If you have questions, contact us through the App Store product page.

第二相机 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年5月25日

关于第二相机

第二相机是设备上的独立图库，与系统「相机」「照片」配合使用：你可以把部分照片和视频单独存放在这里，而不必全部放进系统相册。

1. 信息收集

你拍摄或导入的内容仅保存在本机，我们不会将媒体上传至服务器。

2. 权限使用

  • 相机 — 拍摄照片与录像，保存到独立图库。
  • 麦克风 — 录像时采集声音。
  • 相册 — 从系统相册导入、按需保存回系统相册，以及通过系统分享面板分享。

3. 数据存储

内容保存在应用 Documents 目录。可在设置页查看本地占用与数量。

4. 第三方分享

分享时由 iOS 系统分享面板及你所选应用处理。

5. 联系我们

如有疑问，请通过 App Store 产品页面与我们联系。
