iLocation Privacy Policy

Updated: June 2, 2026

1. Information Collection

This app uses device location services only after you grant permission, to show your current location on the map, resolve addresses, and estimate altitude. Locations you save are stored on your device only and are not uploaded to our servers.

2. Information Use

Location information is used only for map display, coordinate marking, local saving, and sharing within this app.

3. Information Storage

Saved locations are stored as JSON files in the app sandbox Documents directory and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Third-Party Services

Address resolution uses Apple's geocoding service. Sharing is handled by the system share sheet; we do not access your recipients or shared content.

5. Your Rights

You can disable location permission in Settings at any time, or delete saved locations in the app.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us through the App Store listing.
